    About

    Built in Clinton. Proven in Hattiesburg. Headed to Austin.

    Tyler Nichols grew up in Clinton, Mississippi, and went to Clinton High School. At Southern Miss he started as a high jumper who could also long jump. By his junior season he was one of the best long jumpers in the country.

    In 2026 he led the NCAA list in April and won both the long jump and high jump at the Sun Belt Championships. His 8.19m long jump there set both the Sun Belt Conference record and the Southern Miss school record. He made the NCAA final indoors and outdoors and earned Second-Team All-American honors both times. For 2026–27 he is a senior jumper for the Texas Longhorns, majoring in Physical Culture & Sports, and he spent 2026 as a project manager intern at Feniex Industries in Austin.
